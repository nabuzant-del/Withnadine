# Brand tokens and rules

## Identity
- Name: **مع نادين · With Nadine** — handle **@withnadinea**
- Line: **اعرفي وين واقفة / Know where you stand**
- Method: the Three Time Zones, six numbered skills

## Logo
- **Mark:** three shapes in a row — descending half (past), full circle (present), rising half (future).
- **Lockup:** mark + «مع نادين» (Lalezar) + «WITH NADINE» (Figtree 700, tracked .22em).
- Colours: burgundy on light backgrounds, cream/white on dark or burgundy, mustard mark alone on night.
- Clear space: the height of the circle on every side. Minimum: mark 60 px wide on screen, 12 mm in print.
- Don't: recolour the three shapes differently, rotate, stretch, add effects/shadows, put it on busy photos without the tint, or separate «مع نادين» from «WITH NADINE» in the lockup.

| Arabic body (secondary) | Cairo 500 (bold 700 for emphasis) | line-height 1.7 |
| Reel subtitles | Cairo Bold 700 | |

Files: `assets/fonts` (TTF, used by the renderer) and `assets/fonts/otf` (OTF: Lalezar Regular · Cairo Regular/Medium/SemiBold/Bold/ExtraBold/Black · Figtree Regular/Medium/SemiBold/Bold/ExtraBold/Italic). All SIL Open Font License.

No slide counters, page pills or page numbers on carousels.

Don't: use Lalezar for paragraphs · letter-space Arabic · set Arabic in italic · mix more than two Arabic fonts on one slide.

## Shapes
Past = descending half · Present = full circle · Future = rising half. Arranged as arches or single forms. Never decorative icons, emoji, gradients on shapes, or drop shadows.

## Grain
Every background carries grain (dark grain on light, light grain on dark). Flat backgrounds look off-brand.
