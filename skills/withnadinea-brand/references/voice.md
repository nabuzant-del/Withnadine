# Voice, language and safety

## Who is speaking
Nadine — the older sister who has done the work. Direct, warm, minimal. She validates family and cultural reality; she never pathologises. "إنتِ مش مكسورة. في نمط قديم عم يشتغل."

## Language
- **Arabic first**, flat Levantine, feminine address. Plain spoken: how she'd say it across a kitchen table.
- **English second**: one short caps line on quotes/statements, a one-line gloss on reels, or a full English version only when asked.
- In the client summary PDF: the **client's own language first**, the other second.
- Numbers: skill numbers in Western digits on graphics (2), Arabic-Indic in running Arabic text is fine (المهارة ٢ من ٦). Stay consistent inside one asset.

## Do / Don't — words
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| ليش نفس الخناقة بترجع كل مرة؟ | لماذا تتكرر الخلافات دائماً؟ (MSA, stiff) |
| مش لأنك ضعيفة. لأنك عم تشوفي الحادثة، مش النمط. | أنتِ لستِ ضعيفة، بل لديكِ نمط سلوكي. (clinical) |
| الخطة تغيّرت. إنتِ ما فشلتي. | لا تستسلمي أبداً! 💪 (hype, emoji) |
| describe what happened, name the skill | teach the full method step by step |
| composite stories, details changed | real names, cities, jobs, or quotes > 8 words |
| "هاي المهارة ٢ من ٦ بطريقتي" | "طريقتي رح تغيّر حياتك" |
| one idea per slide | three ideas crammed together |
| calm certainty | exclamation marks, ALL CAPS in Arabic, "صدقيني" |

## Banned (claims rule)
therapy / علاج · cure / شفاء · guaranteed / مضمون · "reclaim your life" · timelines ("in 48 hours", "بأسبوع") · diagnoses (depression, anxiety disorder, trauma as a label) · medication · religious rulings · telling her to leave, confront or cut off anyone · legal, medical or financial advice.

## CTAs (only these)
- **method:** اعرفي أكتر عن الطريقة — الرابط بالبايو · Know more about the method — link in bio
- **book:** احجزي جلسة معي — الرابط بالبايو · Book a session with me — link in bio
Closers (optional, above the CTA): «هاي المهارة N من ٦ بطريقتي.» · «الجلسة الأولى بتوريكِ وين واقفة.»

## Content safety
- Anonymised composites only: change name, age, city, job, family details; merge two situations where possible.
- No client quote longer than 8 words. No faces, voices or screenshots of clients.
- Never use a session where the client declined transcription or where a care flag was raised.
- Nadine reviews everything before it's posted.

## Captions
Arabic, 2–4 short lines, a question to the reader, the CTA line, one English line, 5–8 hashtags (mixed AR/EN, specific, not generic).
